Asset Library — Audio

The durable, searchable home for audio assets. Browse, audition, rename, upload, and delete in the panel on the right → Every asset has a stable id and tracks its lineage (what it was derived from). Files live on the 11 TB drive; the index is queried by id.

Scratch — working tier

Transient clips: YouTube rips, filter/separation outputs. Manual cleanup — they stay until you clear them. This is what the panel shows.

Curated — samples & voices

Promoted, kept assets: audio/samples (clips) and audio/voices (refined neutts voices). Promote from scratch when a clip is worth keeping.

The panel is the same control embedded in every audio tool (Voice Filter, Vocal Separation, yt-audio, Audio Normalize) — pick a clip there to flow it into a tool, or manage the library here. Upload a file via the panel's upload button to add a new source asset.
