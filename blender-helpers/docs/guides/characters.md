# Character workflow

Agree the part list and target rig before generation. Keep the base body's under-layer consistent
with the kit. Make each separately generated part an isolated, empty object with a readable
silhouette; inspect remnants, backing surfaces and handedness before image-to-3D.

For multiview input, perfect the front first, then align other views to its canvas, scale and colour.
Check hairstyle, accessories, garment details and limbs across views. Do not accept a plausible
individual image that contradicts the rest of the set.

Fit the body to a protected reference before dressing it. Measure both sides. Classify parts as
rigid, body-following, hanging or mixed; deformation rules differ. Preserve rigid proportions,
conform only intended soft regions, and prefer hidden overlap to fragile joins through detail.
Fit contact to the actual body while preserving the reference's placement and design intent.

Review ring parts along the limb axis, mounted parts edge-on, feet from above and joints from
multiple directions. Use close views for fit and context views for silhouette. Numerical bounds
alone cannot establish skin coverage or a natural-looking joint.

Rig the body separately, using a hierarchy compatible with the intended clips. Place joints from
the actual anatomy, test a first clip early, then use controlled motion strips to diagnose binding.
Change one joint or weighting fault at a time and compare the same motion. Report proportion-
dependent clip limitations rather than deforming the rig to hide them.

Keep models, portraits, motion strips, textures and calibration data in the private job store.
