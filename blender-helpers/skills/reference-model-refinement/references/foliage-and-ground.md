# Foliage, pavers and local ground

Load only for landscape/ground refinement.

- Measure vegetation from local ground to visible foliage top. A core-box height or global Z coordinate is not hedge height. Retain the user's approximate confidence when converting feet to metres.
- Judge shrub setback using the nearest visible foliage and the dirt footprint, not just the trunk origin. Review top and oblique views together; preserve intentional trunk visibility.
- Prefer a coherent compact hedge/core over hidden overlapping shells. Build one card patch first. Check image orientation, internal-gap alpha, contained silhouettes, scale and restrained normal relief before replicating. Opaque rectangles and strong vein normals can look like paper.
- Review at close range and the intended patio/viewing distance. Large stretched leaves and crossed tree planes remain approximations even when the outline matches. More cards alone do not fix a wrong crown silhouette.
- Centre an object on its paver using its footprint/bounds, then check feet contact separately. An object origin may be offset. Distinguish paver thickness from how much protrudes above soil; include joints, fascia clearance and the soil boundary in the review.
- Use video for spatial relationships that still photos hide. Keep estimated slab size, gaps, setback and soil heights labelled as model choices until physically measured.
- Read typed material-preset settings. A field named `stones` was a colour array in one soil preset, not a stone count; a plausible label is not its schema.

Acceptance: relevant scale/footprint checks plus opened detail and context views. Do not claim photo accuracy from a clean topology report or numerical centring alone.
